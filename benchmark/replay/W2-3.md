fixed is resolved by PRism (159 of 159), which is the rollout bar in the audit's policy section;
the share over all roots is bounded by how many findings were ever fixed within the PR's rounds.

Not measurable here: resolution on a posted concession runs through the reply path, which the
replay does not exercise, and the outdated-thread corroboration never fires because the fake
GitHub has no outdated threads.
