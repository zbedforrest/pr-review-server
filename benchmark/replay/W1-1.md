## The 24 same-defect reposts that remain

The harness counts a root as a same-defect repost when an earlier root on the PR has the same file,
a line within ten, and either raw-text Jaccard at or above 0.20 or any one shared contract subject.
Every remaining pair fails the text bar (all 24 are under 0.20, 18 of them at or
above 0.10) and shares a single subject, in every case the enclosing function or component
(`oneclick_join` alone accounts for six). Read side by side they are different points about the
same function, not restatements; the publisher's subject key requires the whole sorted subject set
to match, so it leaves them as separate roots on purpose. Under the publisher's own rule (text at
0.20 with at least two shared words, or the exact subject set) the count is 0. The per-PR target
of 0.05 is therefore not met as the metric is defined; the recommendation is to tighten the
harness's subject clause to the exact subject set, or to shared subject plus Jaccard at or above
0.10, before the next publisher PR reports against it. PRs involved: 17.

## What else changed in the replay

- `fixed` fell from 245 to 174: a row is fixed only when absent and its file changed between the
  head it was last seen at and the current head; the rest stay open and count as still open.
- 43 notes went into existing threads instead of new roots: "Back at <sha7>" when a fixed row
  returned, and one severity line when an aliased finding's cited lines had changed.
- `roots_posted` fell by 31 with no round skipped: every removed root was a repost or a return.


repositories, so keep it outside the repo.
